# DogHouse
Doghouse is CSBay's official moderation discord bot. 


Dog House Privacy Policy
Last updated: 27 September 2026
1. About Dog House
Dog House is a Discord moderation, safety, verification and community-management bot operated for the CSBay (CS2 ⇄ Trading) Discord community.
Dog House processes information about Discord users where necessary to provide moderation, scam prevention, account verification, ticketing, appeals, access control, moderation-evasion prevention and other community-management functionality.
This Privacy Policy explains what information Dog House processes, why it is processed, how it is stored and retained, and the rights and choices available to users.
By participating in CSBay, users should be aware that messages, account information and other activity may be processed by Dog House where necessary to provide these functions.
2. Information We Process
Depending on how a user interacts with CSBay and Dog House, the following categories of information may be processed.
Discord Account and Server Information
Dog House may process:
- Discord user IDs
- Usernames and display names
- Server membership information
- Server roles
- Relevant profile information
- Changes to usernames, display names or profile information
- Server, channel and message IDs
- Dates and timestamps associated with relevant activity
This information is used to identify users within the server and provide moderation, access-control and community-safety functionality.
Messages and Attachments
Dog House may process messages and attachments posted within areas of CSBay in which the bot operates.
This can include:
- Message content
- Images and other attachments
- Links and URLs
- Message author information
- Channel and message IDs
- Message timestamps
- Information derived from automated scam or safety checks
- Limited cached message information required for bot functionality
Dog House may automatically analyse messages and attachments to identify potential scams, suspicious content, rule violations or other activity relevant to community safety.
Moderation Information
When a moderation action is taken, Dog House may process and retain:
- Discord user ID
- Username
- Moderation action
- Moderation reason
- Date and time of the action
- Responsible moderator
- Relevant server roles
- Case identifiers
- Previous relevant moderation history
- Evidence supporting the action
- Appeal history
- Whether an action remains active
- Information concerning attempts to evade an active moderation action
This includes records relating to bans and Dog House restrictions, referred to within CSBay as being "Dogged."
Tickets and Appeals
Dog House operates ticket and appeal functionality.
Information processed through these systems may include:
- Messages submitted by users
- Messages submitted by staff
- Discord usernames and user IDs
- Attachments and links
- Dates and timestamps
- Moderation information
- Information voluntarily supplied by the user
- Information required to investigate or resolve the ticket
Dog House may generate and retain HTML transcripts of tickets and appeals.
Moderation Evidence
Dog House may generate evidence records relating to moderation actions.
These records may contain relevant messages, account information, moderation information and other content necessary to demonstrate why an action was taken.
Evidence may be retained where necessary to enforce or review a moderation action, resolve an appeal, prevent abuse or maintain the integrity of CSBay's moderation system.
Moderation-Evasion Information
Dog House may record attempts to circumvent active moderation restrictions.
This may include:
- Discord user IDs
- Relevant moderation case information
- Rejoin attempts
- Dates and timestamps
- Relevant server activity
This information is used to enforce active restrictions and protect the community against moderation evasion.
Steam Verification Information
Users may voluntarily use Dog House's Steam verification functionality.
When this feature is used, Dog House may process and store:
- Discord user ID
- SteamID64
- Verification date and time
- Last known Steam username/account name
This allows Dog House to associate the Discord account with the Steam account submitted through the verification process.
3. Sources of Information
Most information processed by Dog House is obtained through:
- Discord and the Discord API;
- activity performed by users within CSBay;
- information submitted directly to Dog House;
- information submitted in tickets or appeals;
- actions performed by CSBay moderators; and
- Steam-related information supplied or made available as part of the Steam verification process.
4. How We Use Information
Dog House processes information for purposes including:
- Protecting members from scams and malicious activity
- Detecting suspicious messages, links, images or attachments
- Detecting potential impersonation of staff or trusted community members
- Enforcing CSBay rules
- Recording and administering moderation actions
- Preventing and detecting moderation evasion
- Providing evidence for moderation decisions
- Operating the ticket system
- Operating the appeals system
- Reviewing previous moderation decisions
- Managing server roles and access
- Providing Steam account verification
- Investigating reports and disputes
- Maintaining the security and integrity of Dog House and CSBay
- Diagnosing and maintaining bot functionality
Information collected by Dog House is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.
5. Message Content
Dog House requires access to message content to provide core moderation and community-safety functionality.
Messages and attachments may be automatically processed to identify potentially:
- Fraudulent or scam-related content
- Malicious links
- Suspicious messages
- Rule violations
- Content requiring moderator attention
Where content does not warrant automatic action, Dog House may provide information to authorised moderators for human review.
Message content may also be retained where it forms part of:
- A ticket
- An appeal
- A moderation case
- Moderation evidence
- A safety investigation
Dog House does not use Discord messages or attachments to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
Dog House does not use message content for advertising.
Because message processing is required to provide server-wide moderation, anti-scam and safety functionality, users cannot individually opt out of this processing while participating in areas of CSBay monitored by Dog House.
6. Profile and Impersonation Monitoring
Dog House may process changes to relevant Discord member information to detect potential impersonation or other safety concerns.
For example, changes to usernames, display names or profile information may be compared with information associated with staff members or trusted community members.
Potentially suspicious changes may generate an alert which is provided to authorised CSBay moderators.
An alert does not necessarily mean that the affected user has violated CSBay rules. Where appropriate, a moderator may review the information before deciding whether action is required.
7. Automated Moderation
Some Dog House functions operate automatically.
Depending on the circumstances and configured server rules, Dog House may automatically:
- Detect configured scam or safety indicators
- Remove certain content
- Apply configured server restrictions
- Record moderation information
- Generate evidence
- Alert moderators to potentially suspicious activity
Other situations are referred to moderators for human review.
Users who believe a moderation action was applied incorrectly may use CSBay's ticket or appeals system to request review.
8. Lawful Basis for Processing
Where UK data-protection law applies, Dog House primarily relies upon legitimate interests when processing personal information necessary to operate and protect the CSBay community.
These legitimate interests include:
- Operating and moderating CSBay
- Protecting users from scams and abuse
- Maintaining the security and integrity of the community
- Enforcing community rules
- Maintaining appropriate records of moderation decisions
- Preventing users from circumventing active moderation actions
- Investigating disputes and appeals
Where a user voluntarily initiates a feature, such as opening a ticket, submitting an appeal or linking a Steam account, information is also processed as necessary to provide the functionality requested by that user.
The nature and extent of processing is intended to be proportionate to these purposes.
9. Data Storage
Dog House stores persistent application data on systems used to host and operate the bot.
Stored information may include:
- Moderation records
- Dog House restriction records
- Ban records
- Ticket transcripts
- Appeal transcripts
- Moderation evidence
- Steam verification records
- Moderation-evasion records
- Message/cache information required for bot functionality
Access to stored information is restricted to authorised persons and systems where access is reasonably required to operate, maintain, secure or moderate Dog House and CSBay.
Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.
No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.
10. Data Retention
Dog House retains personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is being processed.
When information is no longer required for Dog House's moderation, safety, verification, ticketing or community-management functionality, it will be deleted or anonymised as soon as reasonably practicable and, under normal circumstances, no later than 90 days after it ceases to be required.
Certain information may remain necessary for longer periods.
For example, moderation records and associated evidence may remain necessary where required to:
- Enforce an active moderation action
- Identify or prevent moderation evasion
- Handle an appeal
- Resolve a dispute
- Establish why a moderation action was taken
- Protect the integrity and safety of the CSBay community
Information associated with an active moderation restriction may therefore remain necessary for the duration of that restriction.
Where information ceases to be necessary for these purposes, the normal deletion or anonymisation process applies.
11. Sharing and Disclosure of Information
Dog House does not sell personal information.
Information processed by Dog House may be accessible to authorised CSBay staff where reasonably necessary to perform moderation, support, appeals, administration or community-safety functions.
Information may also be processed using infrastructure and services necessary to host, maintain or operate Dog House.
Personal information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to respond to a valid legal request.
Access to moderation records, evidence, tickets and similar information is intended to be limited to those who require access for legitimate community-management purposes.
12. Steam Verification
Steam verification is an optional feature initiated by the user.
When a user chooses to verify a Steam account, Dog House stores an association between the user's Discord account and the submitted Steam account.
This information is used for verification, account identification and community-safety purposes.
Steam verification information is not sold or used for advertising.
Users who no longer require their Steam account to be associated with Dog House may submit a privacy request using the contact procedure below. Deletion requests will be considered subject to any continuing legitimate need to retain relevant information.
13. Children's Privacy
Dog House operates within Discord and is not intended to circumvent Discord's age requirements.
Dog House does not intentionally collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable terms and policies.
If CSBay staff become aware that information has been collected in circumstances where it should not have been, appropriate steps may be taken to remove it.
14. Your Data Protection Rights
Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, users may have rights concerning their personal information.
These may include the right to:
- Request access to personal information held about them
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of information
- Request restriction of certain processing
- Object to certain processing
- Receive information about how their personal information is being used
These rights are not absolute and may depend upon the circumstances and lawful basis for processing.
For example, a request to delete information may not require the immediate deletion of a moderation record where that record remains reasonably necessary to enforce an active restriction, prevent moderation evasion, resolve an appeal or protect the community.
Right to Object
Where Dog House relies upon legitimate interests as the lawful basis for processing, users may have the right to object to that processing.
Any objection will be considered based upon the circumstances, the purpose for which the information is being processed, and applicable data-protection requirements.
15. Data Access, Correction and Deletion Requests
Users may contact CSBay to:
- Ask what personal information Dog House holds about them
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of eligible information
- Object to particular processing
- Raise another privacy concern
Requests will be reviewed individually.
CSBay may need to verify the identity of the person making a request before providing, changing or deleting account-related information.
Some information may continue to be retained where there is a lawful and continuing reason to do so.
16. How to Contact Us
Questions, concerns and requests relating to this Privacy Policy or personal information processed by Dog House can be raised directly with CSBay.
To contact us:
1. Join the CSBay Discord server using the permanent invite:
   Join CSBay
2. Head to the #tickets channel.
3. Click the "Other" button to create a ticket.
4. In the ticket, ping @Sudsey and state that the ticket concerns Dog House privacy or personal data.
This process can be used for privacy questions, access requests, correction requests, deletion requests, objections or other concerns regarding information processed by Dog House.
17. Complaints
We encourage users to contact CSBay first if they have concerns about how Dog House processes their information so that the issue can be investigated.
Where UK data-protection law applies, individuals may also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data-protection regulator.

Dog House operates using services provided by third parties, including Discord and, for relevant verification functionality, Steam.
Use of those platforms is separately governed by the privacy policies and terms of their respective operators.
This Privacy Policy describes information processed by Dog House and does not replace the privacy policies of Discord, Steam or other third-party services.
19. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated where:
- Dog House functionality changes
- The types of information processed change
- Data-retention practices change
- Discord's developer requirements change
- Applicable legal or regulatory requirements change
The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be changed when the policy is revised.
Where a material change significantly affects how personal information is processed, reasonable steps will be taken to make users aware of the change.
20. Summary
Dog House processes Discord and related account information for the purpose of operating and protecting the CSBay community.
Its processing primarily supports:
moderation, scam prevention, impersonation detection, tickets, appeals, Steam verification, access management, moderation evidence and prevention of moderation evasion.
Dog House does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use Discord message content to train AI or machine-learning models.
Information is retained only while reasonably necessary for these purposes and is subject to the retention practices described above.
